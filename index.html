Why PFR Flow?

Designed for investors who care about how their money is moving — not about who's watching.

  • Private by design. All data stays on your iPhone — no cloud, no analytics, no accounts.
  • Native iOS experience. Tap-anywhere-to-dismiss-keyboard, hidden tab bars on detail pages, haptics throughout.
  • No third-party charts. Every chart is drawn from scratch for full control of interaction and performance.
  • Multi-currency. Switch between ten major currencies from Settings.
  • Light & dark themes. A refined appearance in either mode, tuned for OLED and LCD alike.
  • One-tap exports. Email a formatted financial snapshot straight from the Reports tab.
