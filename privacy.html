Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

PFR Flow ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal wealth and portfolio companion for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

Short version: PFR Flow stores your financial data locally on your iPhone. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal data through PFR Flow. Specifically, we do not:

  • Create or require an account.
  • Sync your data to any server.
  • Use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks.
  • Sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All financial data you enter into PFR Flow — holdings, transactions, categories, receipts, reports, and settings — is stored locally on your iPhone using standard iOS storage mechanisms (UserDefaults and the app's local sandbox). This data:

  • Never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.
  • Is removed when you delete the app.
  • Is included in standard iOS backups only if you have enabled iCloud or iTunes backups; backup behavior is controlled by Apple, not by us.

Receipt images you attach to transactions are stored inside the app's local sandbox using iOS-standard storage. They are not uploaded anywhere.

3. Permissions We Request

PFR Flow requests only the iOS permissions it needs to function. Each permission is opt-in and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings:

  • Camera — to capture receipt photos for transactions.
  • Photo Library — to attach existing receipt images to transactions.
  • Haptics — to provide tactile feedback for taps and interactions (can be disabled in Settings).

If you decline a permission, the corresponding feature is unavailable, but the rest of the app continues to work.

4. Data You Choose to Export

PFR Flow lets you email a formatted financial snapshot from the Reports tab. When you use this action, the standard iOS share sheet is shown. The email is sent using your default mail app; the contents are composed on your device, and we do not see or store the email.

5. Children

PFR Flow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of PFR Flow after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
